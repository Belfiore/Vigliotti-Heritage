Immigration

Where the Family Came From and How They Arrived

The Vigliotti immigration story is still being researched. No ship list or naturalization paper for a Vigliotti ancestor is in hand yet. Those records are high on the list, especially if the family later wants an Italian citizenship claim.

One related journey is already documented. Pasquale "Pat" Fucale, who married Susan Vigliotti, was born in Cattolica Eraclea, Agrigento, Sicily, on 22 September 1952. At the age of four he and his family immigrated to Montreal, Canada, and about six years later they settled in White Plains, New York. That story is told on his profile and on the family map. It is a Fucale journey, not yet a substitute for the Vigliotti one.

What will go on this page

This page will tell the Vigliotti migration story from the first departure to the first home in a new country, once the records are found.

  • The place of origin: the town or region, what life was like, and why people left.
  • The journey: who left first, the port of departure, ship names and dates.
  • The arrival: port of entry, passenger list records, and the first address.
  • Settling in: jobs, neighborhoods, naturalization, and who followed.
  • Scans of passenger lists, naturalization papers and other documents, each with a caption.
