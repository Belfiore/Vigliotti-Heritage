Immigration

Where the Family Came From and How They Arrived

The Vigliotti, Walsh and Hingston families each crossed an ocean. The records are not all equally firm yet, and nothing here is a claim that anyone is eligible for Italian citizenship.

A New York passenger-list index shows a Vincenzo Vigliotti, aged 17, arriving on the ship Roma on 1 April 1903. The match is probable until the manifest page is read. A member tree points to Santa Maria a Vico and nearby Arienzo, in the province of Caserta, as the Italian hometowns. Those towns are on the map as unverified claims until the civil birth registers are read.

When Vincenzo became a US citizen, if he ever did, is still an open question. A Westchester naturalization index for 1928 and 1930 names a Vincenzo Vigliotti born 2 September 1892 at Bunito, probably Bonito in Avellino. That birth date does not match Vincent of 44 Brambach Avenue, and a second Vincenzo Vigliotti in the county married Nunziata Santonastoso in 1923. Those papers are not treated as his.

On Barbara's side, Michael Walsh was born at Ardmore, Ireland, in 1837 and settled in Yonkers about 1853. Catherine Jane Cleary was born at Huntingdon, Quebec, in 1845 and married Samuel J. Hingston there in 1872; their daughter Clara was probably born at Port Henry, New York, in 1885.

A related journey is also documented. Pasquale "Pat" Fucale, who married Susan Vigliotti, was born in Cattolica Eraclea, Agrigento, Sicily, on 22 September 1952. At the age of four he and his family immigrated to Montreal, Canada, and about six years later they settled in White Plains, New York.

The Places They Left Behind

Place of Origin, unverified

Santa Maria a Vico and Arienzo, Caserta

A member tree names these Campanian towns as the birthplaces of Vincenzo Vigliotti and Vincenza Guida. They are on the family map as unverified claims until the Italian civil registers are read.

Place of Origin, probable

Ardmore, Ireland

Michael Walsh's 1916 obituary says he was born here in 1837. The county is not named; the map uses the village in County Waterford, the usual place of that name.

Place of Origin, probable

Huntingdon, Quebec

Catherine Jane Cleary was born here in 1845, and she and Samuel J. Hingston were married here in 1872.

Arrival, probable

New York, ship Roma, 1 April 1903

A passenger-list index shows Vincenzo Vigliotti, aged 17, arriving on the Roma. The manifest page on NARA roll T715, roll 334, has not yet been found.

First home in hand

44 Brambach Avenue, Eastchester

The 1930 and 1940 censuses, and 1940s newspaper notices, record Vincent and Vincenza's household here.

Related journey

Cattolica Eraclea to Montreal to White Plains

Pat Fucale's obituary describes this Fucale journey. It is on the family map, and is not a substitute for the Vigliotti one.

Status: INDEX IN HAND, manifest not yet read. Arrival at New York on 1 April 1903, age 17. The line on the ship list itself still needs to be found.

Naturalization papers, Vigliotti line

Status: OPEN, identity unproven. A 1928 declaration and 1930 petition in Westchester belong to a Vincenzo Vigliotti born 2 September 1892 at Bunito, and may be a different man. Census citizenship columns and the petition file have not yet been read.

Fucale family journey

Status: IN HAND, from an obituary. Pat Fucale's obituary describes Sicily to Montreal to White Plains. Passenger lists for that journey have not yet been searched.
