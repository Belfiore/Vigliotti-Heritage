Vigliotti

The Vigliotti Family Heritage

This site starts from Dylan Vigliotti, and from his parents, Alphonse John Vigliotti Jr. and Barbara Ehren Walsh. The tree now runs back to Vincenzo Vigliotti, born at Santa Maria a Vico in 1885, and Vincenza Guida, born at Arienzo in 1899, who raised their family at 44 Brambach Avenue in Eastchester, and, on Barbara's side, to Michael Walsh of Ardmore, Ireland, to Margaret Farrell and her parents John Farrell and Morna Fitzgerald, and to the Hingston and Cleary family of Huntingdon, Quebec.

Each fact is labelled with how sure we are. Living people are shown by name and relationship only.

Note: This research is ongoing. Confidence levels are noted throughout.

The Direct Line

From the oldest known ancestors down to Dylan, on both the Vigliotti and Walsh sides.

Generation I, great-great-great-grandparents

Crescenzo and Maria Rosa, the Ruotolos, and the Farrells

Crescenzo Vigliotti and Maria Rosa Carfora are named as Andrea's parents on a probable 1853 birth and again on the 1883 marriage. Raffaele Vigliotti and Pasqualina Carfora are named as Antonia's parents on her 1904 death. Giuseppe Ruotolo and Angela Vigliotti are Mariantonia's probable parents, named on the marriage and on a 1856 birth that fits her age. John Farrell and Morna Fitzgerald are named in a family-history packet as Irish immigrants to Yonkers about 1844, and as Margaret Farrell's parents. Michael Walsh's own parents are not yet named.

Generation II, great-great-grandparents

Andrea and Mariantonia, Alfonso and Antonia, and the Walsh, Hingston and Ehrenfels lines

Andrea Vigliotti and Mariantonia Ruotolo married at the town hall of Santa Maria a Vico, read as 18 February 1883, with the day flagged. Alfonso Guida and Antonia Vigliotti are Vincenza's parents. Michael Walsh, born 1837 at Ardmore, Ireland, was the father of Mayor William Aloysius Walsh. His first wife was Margaret Farrell, named as the mother of John J. Walsh in a 1946 newspaper obituary, and probably also William's mother. Ellen E. Kelly, named in Michael's 1916 obituary, is probably a later wife. Samuel J. Hingston and Catherine Jane Cleary married in Huntingdon, Quebec, in 1872. Augusta R. Ehrenfels of Mount Vernon was the mother of Fernanda Morrisey.

Generation III, great-grandparents

Vincent and Vincenza Vigliotti, Gerard and Susan Mignone, William and Clara Walsh, James and Fernanda Morrisey

Vincent and Vincenza, both born in Italy, raised nine children at 44 Brambach Avenue, Eastchester. Gerard Mignone, also born in Italy, married Susan Rescigno in 1926. William Aloysius Walsh was Mayor of Yonkers from 1926 to 1927, and was probably a son of Margaret Farrell. His older brother John J. Walsh, a banker and ferry president, died in Yonkers in 1946. James T. Morrisey married Fernanda Ehrenfels in 1922.

Generation IV, grandparents

Alphonse J. Vigliotti Sr. and Concetta Mignone; Patricia Morrisey Walsh and Paul H. Walsh

Alphonse Sr. was born 14 March 1921 in Eastchester, married Concetta on 24 August 1947, and died 1 January 2011. Paul was born 7 January 1927 in Yonkers and married Patricia on 5 May 1951.

Generation V, parents

Alphonse John Vigliotti Jr. and Barbara Ehren Walsh

Dylan's parents, and the children of Alphonse Sr. and Concetta, and of Paul and Patricia. Living, so this site records their names and relationship only.
