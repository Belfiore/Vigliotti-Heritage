Vigliotti

The Vigliotti Family Heritage

This site starts from Dylan Vigliotti, and from his parents, Alphonse John Vigliotti and Barbara Ehren Walsh. The first records in hand are two obituaries: one for Pasquale "Pat" Fucale, who married Susan Vigliotti in 1977, and one for Patricia Morrisey Walsh and Paul H. Walsh, who are Barbara's parents.

Research is just beginning. Each fact is labelled with how sure we are, and living people are shown by name and relationship only.

Note: This research is ongoing. Confidence levels are noted throughout.

The Direct Line

From the oldest known ancestors on Barbara's side down to Dylan. Alphonse's parents are not yet known.

Generation I, great-grandparents

Morrisey, Ehrenfels, Walsh and Hingston

James T. Morrisey and Fernanda (Ehrenfels) Morrisey, parents of Patricia. William A. Walsh and Clara (Hingston) Walsh, parents of Paul. Named in the 2019 Walsh obituary.

Generation II, grandparents

Patricia Morrisey Walsh and Paul H. Walsh

Married 5 May 1951. Patricia was born in Bronxville and died on 4 September 2019. Paul was born in Yonkers and died on 27 September 2019. They raised their children in Larchmont and later lived in Rye.

Generation III, parents

Alphonse John Vigliotti and Barbara Ehren Walsh

Dylan's parents. Living, so this site records their names and relationship only.
