The Vigliotti Family Heritage

This site starts from Dylan Vigliotti, and from his parents, Alphonse John Vigliotti Jr. and Barbara Ehren Walsh. The tree now runs back to Vincenzo Vigliotti, born at Santa Maria a Vico in 1885, and Vincenza Guida, born at Arienzo in 1899, who raised their family at 44 Brambach Avenue in Eastchester, and, on Barbara's side, to Michael Walsh of Ardmore, Ireland, and to the Hingston and Cleary family of Huntingdon, Quebec.

Each fact is labelled with how sure we are. Living people are shown by name and relationship only.

Note: This research is ongoing. Confidence levels are noted throughout.

The Direct Line

From the oldest known ancestors down to Dylan, on both the Vigliotti and Walsh sides.

Generation I, great-great-grandparents

Walsh of Ardmore, Hingston and Cleary of Huntingdon, and Ehrenfels

Michael Walsh, born 1837 at Ardmore, Ireland, settled in Yonkers and was the father of Mayor William Aloysius Walsh. Samuel J. Hingston and Catherine Jane Cleary married in Huntingdon, Quebec, in 1872 and were the parents of Clara Hingston. Augusta R. Ehrenfels of Mount Vernon was the mother of Fernanda Morrisey.

Generation II, great-grandparents

Vincent and Vincenza Vigliotti, Gerard and Susan Mignone, William and Clara Walsh, James and Fernanda Morrisey

Vincent and Vincenza, both born in Italy, raised nine children at 44 Brambach Avenue, Eastchester. Gerard Mignone, also born in Italy, married Susan Rescigno in 1926. William Aloysius Walsh was Mayor of Yonkers from 1926 to 1927. James T. Morrisey married Fernanda Ehrenfels in 1922.

Generation III, grandparents

Alphonse J. Vigliotti Sr. and Concetta Mignone; Patricia Morrisey Walsh and Paul H. Walsh

Alphonse Sr. was born 14 March 1921 in Eastchester, married Concetta on 24 August 1947, and died 1 January 2011. Paul was born 7 January 1927 in Yonkers and married Patricia on 5 May 1951.

Generation IV, parents

Alphonse John Vigliotti Jr. and Barbara Ehren Walsh

Dylan's parents, and the children of Alphonse Sr. and Concetta, and of Paul and Patricia. Living, so this site records their names and relationship only.
