Records, Sources and Next Steps

Every claim on this site should be traceable to a source in data/sources.json. Living people are recorded by name and relationship only. Current addresses named in the records have been held back. Italian hometowns from a member tree, and a 1928 to 1930 naturalization index for a Vincenzo Vigliotti, are recorded as open questions, not as settled facts.

Documents In Hand

Obituary of Pasquale P. Fucale

Status: IN HAND. Ballard-Durand, after his death on 21 November 2026. Confirms his birth in Cattolica Eraclea, his marriage to Susan Vigliotti on 30 October 1977, and names Alphonse Vigliotti (Barbara) and Renee Makras (George) as in-laws. Original page.

Obituaries: Patricia and Paul Walsh

Status: IN HAND. The Rye Record, 14 October 2019. Confirms Patricia and Paul, their marriage on 5 May 1951, their parents, and their daughter Barbara Walsh Vigliotti and her husband Al. Original page.

Family testimony: Dylan's parents

Status: IN HAND. Sebastian named Barbara Ehren Walsh (maiden) and Alphonse John Vigliotti as Dylan's parents. Two obituaries, including one that names Dylan as a grandchild of Alphonse Sr., now confirm that parentage.

Obituary of Alphonse J. Vigliotti Sr.

Status: IN HAND. The Journal News, 3 January 2011, transcribed on Find a Grave memorial 63661294. Gives his birth, death, parents, wife, children (Alphonse Jr., Susan Fucale, Renee Makras), and grandchildren including Dylan and Ehren, and confirms the sibling link. Memorial.

World War II draft card, 15 February 1942

Status: INDEX IN HAND. Alphonse John Vigliotti, born 14 March 1921 at Scarsdale, registered at Tuckahoe, naming Vincent Vigliotti as next of kin. The card image has not yet been fully captured.

1930 and 1940 censuses, 44 Brambach Avenue

Status: INDEX IN HAND. Vincent and Vincenza (Winnie) Vigliotti, both born in Italy, with their children including Alphonse, at 44 Brambach Avenue, Eastchester. Full page images have not yet been read line by line.

Scarsdale Inquirer family notices, 1942 to 1957

Status: IN HAND. Four sons in the service (4 December 1942), Vincent's death at Grasslands Hospital (28 April 1944), the Mignone-Vigliotti wedding (24 August 1947), Augusta Ehrenfels's death (8 June 1949), the Walsh-Morrisey wedding (5 May 1951), and Gerard Mignone's death (29 October 1957). Texts from HRVH; some page images were blocked by a human check.

New York State death index, 24 April 1944

Status: IN HAND. Vincent Vigliotti, indexed as VIGHIOTTO, died at Mount Pleasant, certificate 26490. Combined with the newspaper notice, this is treated as Vincent of 44 Brambach Road.

William Aloysius Walsh and the Walsh-Hingston line

Status: IN HAND. Find a Grave memorial 101118568, with the inscription Mayor 1926 1927, and obituaries of Michael Walsh (Yonkers Statesman, 10 July 1916) and Catherine Jane Cleary Hingston (Herald Statesman, 1 September 1932). These take the Walsh line to Ardmore, Ireland, and the Hingston line to Huntingdon, Quebec.

Westchester marriage indexes, 1922 and 1926

Status: INDEX IN HAND. Certificate 37675, James T. Morrisey and Fernanda Ehrenfels, 1922. Certificate 51289, Gerardo Mignone and Susan M. Rescigno, 1926. The certificates themselves have not been ordered.

Passenger-list index, ship Roma, 1 April 1903

Status: INDEX IN HAND, manifest not yet read. Vincenzo Vigliotti, age 17, arrived at New York on the Roma. NARA roll T715, roll 334, has been located; the handwritten line has not yet been found.

Italian citizenship law, Judgment 63/2026

Status: LEGAL SOURCE IN HAND. Law 74/2025 and Constitutional Court Judgment 63/2026 set tighter conditions for jure sanguinis claims. This is recorded so the question can be discussed accurately. This site does not claim that anyone in the family is eligible. A lawyer would need to assess the line.

Leads To Follow

Italian birth registers for Santa Maria a Vico and Arienzo

Status: LEAD, from a member tree. The KINDRED SPIRITS tree (65434896) gives Vincenzo born 4 July 1885 at Santa Maria a Vico, son of Andrea Vigliotti and Mariantonia Ruotolo, and Vincenza Pasqualina Guida born 21 September 1899 at Arienzo. Those acts have not been found yet. Vincenzo's birth year is also in conflict with census ages, his 1944 death notice, and an unrelated naturalization index.

Vincenzo's US naturalization, if any

Status: OPEN, identity unproven. A 1928 declaration and 1930 petition name a Vincenzo Vigliotti born 2 September 1892 at Bunito (probably Bonito, Avellino). That is probably a different man, perhaps the Vincenzo who married Nunziata Santonastoso in 1923 (certificate 40566). Census citizenship columns and petition document 18337 would settle this. If our Vincent never naturalized, or only after March 1921, that would matter for any later citizenship claim.

Marriage of Vincent and Vincenza

Status: NOT YET FOUND. A 1942 notice of a twenty-fifth anniversary points to 1916 or 1917. A member-tree date of 31 July 1916 was not found in the Westchester, New York City, or 1916 New York State marriage indexes. Parish registers, the 1917 state index G pages, or a marriage in Italy remain to be checked.

Vital records still to order or read

Status: NEXT STEPS. Birth certificates for Alphonse Sr. (14 March 1921) and Paul Walsh (7 January 1927); death certificate 26490 for Vincent, 1944; Concetta's November 2012 obituary or death record; the 1949 death-index image for Augusta Ehrenfels; the 19 March 1967 New York Times obituary of Mayor Walsh; and the 1872 Huntingdon marriage register. No paid order will be placed without approval.

Held back

  • Current or recent homes of living people, including a 2019 residence named in the Walsh obituary.
  • Dates and addresses for anyone who is or may be living, including siblings of Alphonse Sr. who were named as surviving him in 2011, until a death record is in hand.
  • Grandchildren named only by first name, other than Dylan and Ehren. Ehren is on the tree as a probable child of Alphonse Jr. and Barbara.
  • A 1969 engagement notice naming an Alphonse J. Vigliotti Jr. and a Patricia Patton. That is an unverified lead, and it is not attached to the tree.
  • The unclear wording in Pat's obituary about Josephine Scopelliti. Josephine is on the tree as Pat's sister; no spouse is recorded until that is clear.
