Records, Sources and Next Steps

Every claim on this site should be traceable to a source in data/sources.json. Living people are recorded by name and relationship only. Current addresses named in the records have been held back. Vincenzo's 1885 birth at Santa Maria a Vico and Vincenza's 1899 birth at Arienzo are confirmed by the civil acts. His naturalization in 1922 or 1923 is probable, pending a reading of the petition.

Documents In Hand

Obituary of Pasquale P. Fucale

Status: IN HAND. Ballard-Durand, after his death on 21 November 2026. Confirms his birth in Cattolica Eraclea, his 1977 marriage to a daughter of the Eastchester Vigliotti family, and names her brother and sister, with their spouses, as his in-laws. Original page.

Obituaries: Patricia and Paul Walsh

Status: IN HAND. The Rye Record, 14 October 2019. Confirms Patricia and Paul, their marriage on 5 May 1951, their parents, and their daughter and her husband. Original page.

Family testimony: Dylan's parents

Status: IN HAND. Sebastian named Barbara Ehren Walsh (maiden) and Alphonse John Vigliotti as Dylan's parents. Two obituaries, including one that names Dylan as a grandchild of Alphonse Sr., now confirm that parentage.

Obituary of Alphonse J. Vigliotti Sr.

Status: IN HAND. The Journal News, 3 January 2011, transcribed on Find a Grave memorial 63661294. Gives his birth, death, parents, wife, a son, two daughters, and grandchildren, and confirms the sibling link among his children. Memorial.

World War II draft card, 15 February 1942

Status: INDEX IN HAND. Alphonse John Vigliotti, born 14 March 1921 at Scarsdale, registered at Tuckahoe, naming Vincent Vigliotti as next of kin. The card image has not yet been fully captured.

1930 and 1940 censuses, 44 Brambach Avenue

Status: INDEX IN HAND. Vincent and Vincenza (Winnie) Vigliotti, both born in Italy, with their children including Alphonse, at 44 Brambach Avenue, Eastchester. The 1930 and 1940 images have now been read for the citizenship columns (Na). The 1920 and 1925 censuses were also read: first papers in 1920, and a citizen from 1923 at White Plains in 1925.

Scarsdale Inquirer family notices, 1942 to 1957

Status: IN HAND. Four sons in the service (4 December 1942), Vincent's death at Grasslands Hospital (28 April 1944), the Mignone-Vigliotti wedding (24 August 1947), Augusta Ehrenfels's death (8 June 1949), the Walsh-Morrisey wedding (5 May 1951), and Gerard Mignone's death (29 October 1957). Texts from HRVH; some page images were blocked by a human check.

New York State death index, 24 April 1944

Status: IN HAND. Vincent Vigliotti, indexed as VIGHIOTTO, died at Mount Pleasant, certificate 26490. Combined with the newspaper notice, this is treated as Vincent of 44 Brambach Road.

William Aloysius Walsh and the Walsh-Hingston line

Status: IN HAND. Find a Grave memorial 101118568, with the inscription Mayor 1926 1927, and obituaries of Michael Walsh (Yonkers Statesman, 10 July 1916) and Catherine Jane Cleary Hingston (Herald Statesman, 1 September 1932). These take the Walsh line to Ardmore, Ireland, and the Hingston line to Huntingdon, Quebec.

Westchester marriage indexes, 1922 and 1926

Status: INDEX IN HAND. Certificate 37675, James T. Morrisey and Fernanda Ehrenfels, 1922. Certificate 51289, Gerardo Mignone and Susan M. Rescigno, 1926. The certificates themselves have not been ordered.

Passenger list, ship Roma, 1 April 1903

Status: MANIFEST READ, match probable. Sheet 18, line 18, a 17-year-old farm labourer from Naples, probably Vincenzo Vigliotti, going to a cousin in Newburgh. The surname is only partly readable.

Vincenzo's civil birth act, Santa Maria a Vico, 1885

Status: IN HAND, FACT. Act 131, registered 5 July 1885 by his father Andrea Vigliotti, aged 32. Vincenzo was born at 11 at night on 4 July 1885 at Via Rusciano 23, to Andrea and Mariantonia Ruotolo. A highlighted copy is on his profile. Highlighted page.

Vincenza's civil birth act, Arienzo, 1899, and her twin

Status: IN HAND, FACT. Act 105, registered 23 September 1899 by Alfonso Guida, aged 33. Vincenza Pasqualina was born at 11:40 in the morning on 21 September 1899 at via Maddalena 2, the first of twins; her brother Pasquale Fabrizio was born second. Highlighted page.

Antonia Vigliotti's civil death act, Arienzo, 1904

Status: IN HAND, FACT. Act 29, registered 9 June 1904. Antonia, wife of Alfonso Guida, died on 8 June 1904, aged 38. The act says she was born at San Felice a Cancello, daughter of Raffaele Vigliotti and Pasqualina Carfora (those parent names are probable). Highlighted page.

Andrea Vigliotti's civil birth act, 1853

Status: IN HAND, identification PROBABLE. Act 158, Santa Maria a Vico, 30 November 1853, son of Crescenzo Vigliotti and Maria Rosa Carfora, of Strada Rusciano. He is probably Vincenzo's father: the only Andrea Vigliotti in the 1852 and 1853 registers, and the same street. Highlighted page.

Vincenzo's naturalization, 1916 and 1922

Status: INDEX AND CENSUS IN HAND, PROBABLE. Declaration 1916 (indexed Viglietto) and petition 1922 (indexed Vigletti, also Viglotti), with the 1920 first-papers column and the 1925 note 1923 White Plains. The petition itself has not yet been read. The 1928 and 1930 papers in the same index belong to a different Vincenzo, born in 1892 at Bonito.

Leads To Follow

A possible birth for Mariantonia Ruotolo, 1856

Status: LEAD. Maria Antonia Ruotolo was born 28 June 1856 on Strada Rusciano, Santa Maria a Vico, daughter of Giuseppe Ruotolo and Angela Vigliotti. A later note on the act records a marriage to an Alfonso P. in 1896, or possibly 1876. She is a candidate for Vincenzo's mother, not a proven identity, until Andrea and Mariantonia's marriage act is read. Highlighted page.

Andrea's marriage act, about 1875 to 1884

Status: NOT YET FOUND. The marriage of Andrea Vigliotti and Mariantonia Ruotolo would name his parents and settle whether he is the child born in 1853, and would also settle whether the 1856 Maria Antonia is Vincenzo's mother.

Antonia Vigliotti's birth at San Felice a Cancello

Status: NEXT STEP, PROBABLE as it stands. Her 1904 death act gives the town and her parents, Raffaele Vigliotti and Pasqualina Carfora. A birth act around 1866 would confirm them.

Marriage of Vincent and Vincenza

Status: NOT YET FOUND. A 1942 notice of a twenty-fifth anniversary points to 1916 or 1917. A member-tree date of 31 July 1916 was not found in the Westchester, New York City, or 1916 New York State marriage indexes. Parish registers, the 1917 state index G pages, or a marriage in Italy remain to be checked. Both families include the surnames Vigliotti and Carfora, so they may have been cousins; that is a lead only, not a fact.

Vital records still to order or read

Status: NEXT STEPS. Birth certificates for Alphonse Sr. (14 March 1921) and Paul Walsh (7 January 1927); death certificate 26490 for Vincent, 1944; Concetta's November 2012 obituary or death record; the 1949 death-index image for Augusta Ehrenfels; the 19 March 1967 New York Times obituary of Mayor Walsh; and the 1872 Huntingdon marriage register. No paid order will be placed without approval.

Held back

  • Current or recent homes of living people, including a 2019 residence named in the Walsh obituary.
  • Dates and addresses for anyone who is or may be living, including siblings of Alphonse Sr. who were named as surviving him in 2011, until a death record is in hand.
  • Grandchildren named only by first name, other than Dylan and Ehren. Ehren is on the tree as a probable child of Alphonse Jr. and Barbara.
  • A 1969 engagement notice naming an Alphonse J. Vigliotti Jr. and a Patricia Patton. That is an unverified lead, and it is not attached to the tree.
  • The unclear wording in Pat's obituary about Josephine Scopelliti. Josephine is on the tree as Pat's sister; no spouse is recorded until that is clear.
