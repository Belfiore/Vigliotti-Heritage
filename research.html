Records, Sources and Next Steps

Every claim on this site should be traceable to a source in data/sources.json. Living people are recorded by name and relationship only. Current addresses named in the records have been held back.

Documents In Hand

Obituary of Pasquale P. Fucale

Status: IN HAND. Ballard-Durand Funeral and Cremation Services, after his death on 21 November 2026. Confirms his birth on 22 September 1952 in Cattolica Eraclea, Sicily, his marriage to Susan Vigliotti of Eastchester, New York, on 30 October 1977, and names Alphonse Vigliotti (Barbara) and Renee Makras (George) as brothers- and sisters-in-law. Original page.

Obituaries: Patricia and Paul Walsh

Status: IN HAND. The Rye Record, published 14 October 2019. Confirms Patricia Morrisey Walsh (died 4 September 2019) and Paul H. Walsh (died 27 September 2019), their marriage on 5 May 1951, their parents, and their daughter Barbara Walsh Vigliotti and her husband Al. Original page.

Family testimony: Dylan's parents

Status: IN HAND. Sebastian named Barbara Ehren Walsh (maiden) and Alphonse John Vigliotti as Dylan Vigliotti's parents, and asked that they be the starting point of this research. Until a birth record is in hand, that parentage is labelled family tradition, with support from the two obituaries that name the couple.

Leads To Follow

Birth and marriage records for Alphonse, Barbara, Susan and Dylan

Status: NOT YET SEARCHED. Needed to confirm parentage in the civil record, to name Alphonse's and Susan's parents, and to support any later Italian citizenship claim.

Italian origin of the Vigliotti line

Status: NOT YET SEARCHED. A town of origin, a ship list and naturalization papers are still to find. Pat Fucale's Sicilian origin is known; the Vigliotti town is not.

Sibling link between Susan, Alphonse and Renee

Status: PROBABLE, from in-law wording. Pat's obituary lists Alphonse Vigliotti (Barbara) and Renee Makras (George) as brothers- and sisters-in-law, after naming Pat's own sisters separately. A birth record would make the sibling link firm.

Walsh, Morrisey, Ehrenfels and Hingston vital records

Status: NOT YET SEARCHED. Exact birth dates for Patricia and Paul, burial places, and earlier generations on both sides.
